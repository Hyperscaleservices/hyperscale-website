HYPERSCALE / SAFETY

Safety.

Hyperscale is committed to protecting the health, safety, and well-being of our employees, clients, contractors, visitors, business partners, the public, and the environment. Safety is the foundation of every decision we make, every project we execute, and every relationship we build.

Environmental, Health & Safety

Management System

Our environmental, health, and safety approach sets expectations for industrial fabrication, construction, and maintenance. Planning, field supervision, and hazard controls help teams manage risks throughout the work.

Our Commitment

Beyond Compliance

We incorporate safe work practices into planning and execution. We expect everyone to understand and actively support the controls that protect people, property, and the environment.

Every Employee

Stop Work Authority

Every employee has the authority and obligation to identify hazards, intervene when unsafe conditions exist, and exercise Stop Work Authority whenever necessary.

Our Standard

Every Incident Is Preventable

Through planning, disciplined execution, employee engagement, leadership involvement, and continuous improvement, we work to prevent incidents across our operations.
